# Decision log

One entry per decision: date, decision, reason.

- Code lives in files and syncs to Studio with Rojo. Studio is a viewer only.
- 2026-11-17: Two places. A light entry place routes players; every campaign is a reserved server of the campaign place, so an empty campaign can freeze and be re-entered later (public servers can't be re-entered).
- 2026-11-17: Marks save in one DataStore record per player with a session lock (UpdateAsync; lock refreshed by every autosave, stale after 150 s). Saves merge with what is stored, so marks never go down. A separate ordered store keeps each player's highest marks and is re-applied on load.
- 2026-11-17: The 100-mark release code is granted once when the player finishes the menu guide. The guide opens on first join and can be closed and reopened. No tutorial place.
- 2026-11-17: One marks gate per item: unlocked = equippable = craftable (with the right bench, class and server condition).
- 2026-11-17: Auto-match: friends first, then fill the fullest server to ~20, then the emptiest under ~45, then wake the newest frozen campaign; a new Day 0 only when nothing is frozen.
- 2026-11-17: Public campaigns end after 24 h frozen. Private servers' campaigns freeze but never end; the owner can reset to Day 0.
- 2026-11-19 (superseded below): The map greybox is generated from data. `src/shared/Map/Data.luau` holds every coordinate (coasts, hills, roads, places); `lune run tools/greybox/build` turns it into `assets/Greybox.rbxm` (plain anchored SmoothPlastic parts, coloured by purpose), which Rojo puts in Workspace. Move a place by editing Data, then rebuild. Roads take the uncut ridge height, so the artery crosses the gorge on a bridge.
- 2026-11-19: Greybox colours: light grey buildings and area footprints, dark grey roads, red enemy sites, blue player bases, yellow pickups, purple gated zones and keypads, orange radio/power/cut points; ground tan (autumn), pale blue (frost band), white (winter), grey (rock). A legend board stands at HQ 1.
- 2026-11-19: Player metrics for every block live in `src/shared/Map/Metrics.luau` (R6 5 tall, vault 3, crouch cover 3.5, prone 1.5, door 4 x 7.5, stairs 35 degrees, track 10 / road 20 / artery 28, max drivable slope 30 degrees).
- 2026-11-19: Map scaled 2.5x to 20,480 x 17,920 studs (~5.7 x 5.0 km), centred on the origin (world X = x - 10,240, Z = y - 8,960). Towns and sites keep their size and the land between them grows; items keep their place inside their site. HQ 1 to HQ 2 is ~5:46 by truck, ~13 min on foot.
- 2026-11-20: Map cut back again to 1.5x: 12,288 x 10,752 studs (~3.4 x 3.0 km), cell 768 (still c01-c16 / r01-r14), world X = x - 6,144, Z = y - 5,376. 2.0x still felt too big. Mountain peaks scaled by the same 0.75 so slopes don't turn into cliffs. HQs stay 500 studs. HQ 1 to HQ 2 is ~3:27 by truck, ~7:46 on foot.
- 2026-11-19: Map cut back to 2.0x: 16,384 x 14,336 studs (~4.6 x 4.0 km), cell 1,024 (still c01-c16 / r01-r14), world X = x - 8,192, Z = y - 7,168. 2.5x felt too big. HQ 1 to HQ 2 is ~4:37 by truck, ~10:22 on foot. HQs are 500-stud walled compounds (r = 250 in Data), the central points of the game.
- 2026-11-19: The ground is Roblox terrain, generated from the same maths as the parts (`src/shared/Map/Geo.luau`: coast distance, rolling noise hills, ridged mountains, carved lake / river / gorge, flat town pads, road beds capped at 14 degrees with shared junction heights). `tools/terrain/generate.luau` writes it in a Studio copy (~2.5 min), `tools/terrain/extract` saves it to `assets/Terrain.rbxl` (LFS, ~13 MB at 1.5x), and `tools/terrain/merge` copies it into every build (`scripts/check.sh` does this). Rojo can't carry terrain.
- 2026-11-19: Real terrain materials as placeholders: snow, slate and rock by slope and height; grass, leafy grass and bare fields in autumn sector B; patchy snow in the frost band; asphalt roads, dirt tracks, sand beaches, real water. Muted material colours are set by the generator.
- 2026-11-19: Placeholder buildings are solid blocks (no interiors): towns on a rough street grid (taller toward the middle of North City), bases with wall rings and barracks blocks, HQ walls in blue. Road decks span water and the gorge.
